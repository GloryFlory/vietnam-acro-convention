About the Festival

The First Acroyoga
Festival in Vietnam

Welcome to the Vietnam Acro Convention — a landmark celebration of movement, connection, and community set against the breathtaking backdrop of Hoi An's ancient lantern-lit streets.

We're bringing together a world-class teaching faculty from across the globe, passionate practitioners, and the timeless spirit of Vietnam for three unforgettable days.

Whether you're discovering Acroyoga for the first time or exploring the heights of Standing Acro, VAC 2027 has a track crafted to elevate your practice — and your spirit.

The Destination

The Magic of
Hoi An

Hoi An is one of Southeast Asia's most enchanting destinations — a UNESCO World Heritage town where ancient temples, lantern-lit streets, and the gentle Thu Bon River create an atmosphere unlike anywhere else on earth.

Practising Acroyoga in this setting is more than exercise — it's a full immersive experience. After workshops, wander the ancient town on foot, feast on world-class Vietnamese cuisine, or take a short ride to pristine An Bang Beach.

March brings warm, dry weather — 24–28°C with blue skies. Perfect conditions for three days of focused practice and sunset adventures.

Lantern Festival Magic

Hoi An's monthly Full Moon Lantern Festival fills the river with floating lights and the streets with colour. Simply breathtaking.

UNESCO Ancient Town

Walk through 2,000 years of history in Hoi An's remarkably preserved old quarter — a testament to centuries of cultural exchange.

A Food Paradise

Cao Lau, White Rose dumplings, Bánh Mì, fresh seafood — Hoi An is consistently ranked among the world's great food destinations.

Beach Just 5 km Away

An Bang Beach — one of Vietnam's most beautiful stretches of sand — is a short bike ride from the ancient town.

Easy to Reach

Da Nang International Airport is 30 minutes away, with direct connections to Singapore, Bangkok, Seoul, and beyond.

Ideal March Weather

Warm, dry, and sunny at 24–28°C. The absolute best time to visit Central Vietnam.

Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Planning your trip to VAC 2027? Here are answers to the most common questions.

What is the Vietnam Acro Convention?

VAC 2027 is the first dedicated Acroyoga festival in Vietnam, taking place March 5–7, 2027 in the ancient town of Hoi An. We're bringing together a world-class teaching faculty and a growing community of practitioners for three days of workshops, jams, and deep connection.

What level do I need to attend?

Everyone is welcome. We run 4 parallel tracks — Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, and Standing Acro — so whether you've never tried Acroyoga or you're a seasoned practitioner, there's a track made for exactly where you are. You can also move between tracks throughout the weekend.

Do I need to come with a partner?

Not at all. Acroyoga is a community practice and partner rotation is encouraged and celebrated at festivals. You'll be paired with other practitioners throughout the weekend. Solo travellers are very welcome and always fit right in.

How do I get to Hoi An?

Fly into Da Nang International Airport (DAD), approximately 30 minutes from Hoi An by taxi or shuttle. Da Nang has direct connections from Singapore, Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur, Seoul, and many more Asian hubs. Full travel guidance will be published closer to the event.

Where will the festival take place?

We are currently selecting a venue in and around Hoi An, with a focus on spaces that connect practitioners to nature and the unique beauty of the area. Full venue details will be announced in 2026.

Is accommodation included in the ticket?

Accommodation is not included in the festival pass. Hoi An has options for every budget — from charming riverside guesthouses to boutique hotels and luxury resorts. We'll share a curated accommodation guide closer to ticket sales.

When do tickets go on sale?

Tickets will go on sale in 2026. We'll offer an exclusive early bird window for everyone on our interest list first — so register your interest now to access the best price before general sale opens.

I'm interested in teaching at VAC 2027 — how can I apply?

We're curating our teaching faculty for 2027. If you're an established teaching couple with a strong community presence, we'd love to hear from you. Register via the form on this page, select "Teacher / Facilitator" as your level, and we'll reach out directly.
